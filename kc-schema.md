# Knowledge Base Schema for Adventist Bible Study Tool

## Overview

This defines the structure for all materials stored in the knowledge base. Each entry is a Markdown file with YAML frontmatter for metadata.

## Entry Schema

```yaml
---
id: unique-identifier (e.g., "gen-1-1-kjv")
type: material/bible | material/spirit-of-prophecy | material/commentary | material/original-language | material/lexicon | material/translation | material/study-guide
book: book/genesis | book/exodus | ... | book/revelation | book/ot | book/nt | book/gospels | book/pauline
passage: "Genesis 1:1" or "Genesis 1" (optional for non-passage materials)
tags:
  - theme/sanctuary
  - lang/hebrew
  - strongs-H6662
  - translation/kjv
  - xref/parallel
source: source/bible | source/ellen-white | source/commentary | ...
language: hebrew | greek | aramaic | english
translation: kjv | nkjv | esv | niv | csb | nlt | lxx | masoretic
level: intro | intermediate | advanced
status: draft | review | final | needs-update
created: YYYY-MM-DD
updated: YYYY-MM-DD
related:
  - id: related-entry-id-1
  - id: related-entry-id-2
cross_references:
  - type: xref/parallel | xref/fulfillment | xref/theme | xref/contrast | xref/word-study | xref/spirit-prophecy
    target: "related-passage or entry id"
    note: "Optional explanation of the relationship"
semantic_links:
  - sl-001
  - sf-power-strength
historical_context:             # Optional (ADR-022 Option A). For curated, high-value passages only.
  era: "Second Temple period (circa 516 BC – AD 70)"
  location: "Jerusalem, Judea — under Roman occupation"
  cultural_note: "Brief plain-English note on the cultural, political, or geographical circumstance directly relevant to this verse."
  source: "Attributable scholarly or Spirit of Prophecy source (e.g., Josephus, Antiquities 18.1.3; DA 167.1)"
---

# Content Title

## Content Body

The actual text, commentary, word study, or material content goes here.

## Notes
Optional study notes, observations, or questions.

## Greek/Hebrew Word Study (if applicable)
- **Original Word**: Hebrew/Greek text
- **Strong's Number**: H#### or G####
- **Transliteration**: transliterated form
- **Definition**: Core meaning
- **Usage Count**: How many times it appears in the Bible
- **Semantic Range**: Range of meanings across contexts
- **Key Passages**: Other passages where this word appears

## Correlations
- Link to related passages, themes, and Spirit of Prophecy references
- Cross-language connections (Hebrew → Greek → English)

## AI-Generated Insights (optional)
- Summaries, explanations, or connections generated by the AI
- Marked as AI-generated for transparency
```

## Metadata Rules

1.  Every entry MUST have a unique `id`
    
2.  `id` format: `{book}-{chapter}-{verse}-{source}` or `{category}-{name}-{source}`
    
3.  All tags must be from the taxonomy (see Tag Taxonomy)
    
4.  `related` field links to other entry IDs for quick navigation
    
5.  `cross_references` field provides structured relationship types
    
6.  `semantic_links` field references relationship IDs from `semantic-links.json`
    
7.  AI-generated content MUST be marked with `<!-- AI-GENERATED -->` comment
    
8.  All files are UTF-8 encoded Markdown with YAML frontmatter

9.  `historical_context` is an **optional** block for curated, high-value passages (ADR-022 Option A).
    Sub-fields: `era`, `location`, `cultural_note`, `source`. The `source` must be an
    attributable published source (Josephus, BRI, Patriarchs and Prophets, etc.) — not AI
    speculation. This field illuminates the text; it never overrides or subordinates Scripture.
    Historical-critical presuppositions (denial of miracles, secular source theories) are
    prohibited per the 1986 SDA "Methods of Bible Study" and ADR-001.
