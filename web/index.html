Step 1: Pinned Data Integrity

The study workstation operates 100% offline from cryptographically pinned SQLite databases and lexicons. Every artifact is verified against authoritative SHA-256 checksums before study begins.

● Verifying sidecar data bundle…
  • Scripture DB bible.db — 66 books, 31,102 verses (KJV + alignments)
  • Original Languages macula.db — Macula Hebrew OT & Greek NT morphology
  • Lexicons lexicons/ — Strong's Hebrew/Greek concordances & apparatus
ADR-006 & ADR-024: Derived data files are audited deterministically so you never have to worry about data corruption or silent alterations.
