## Study Notes

<!-- AI-GENERATED -->
*   **"To Rule" (*limshol*, H4910) — Dominion as Service:** The luminaries "rule" the day and the night — but their governance consists of giving light (v15, v17). Rule in the creation order is stewardship exercised on behalf of others, the pattern later extended to humanity (v26-28; cf. Psalm 8:6). The noun *memshalah* (H4475, "rule, dominion") in v16 and the verb *mashal* (H4910) here bind the two halves of Day 4 together: light and rule are inseparable.
*   **The Division Motif Completed (H914; H216; H2822):** "To divide the light from the darkness" is the third movement of the creation division — light from darkness (v4), waters from waters (v6-7), and now the spheres of day and night under their appointed rulers. Day and night are ordered domains, not cosmic forces to be feared; the astral realm that terrified neighboring cultures is here a well-run household.
*   **"And God Saw That It Was Good" (H7200; H2896):** The divine appraisal closes Day 4 — the fourth "good" pronouncement (v4, v10, v12, v18). What surrounding nations worshipped is declared *tov*: a well-made creature under the one true God.
