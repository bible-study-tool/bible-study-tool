*   **The *Protevangelium* — The First Gospel Announcement:** Genesis 3:15 is universally recognized across Christian and Adventist theology (NOTICE.md) as the primeval fountain of redemptive prophecy: the *Protevangelium*. Spoken in the hearing of the fallen pair before their expulsion from Eden, it transformed the judgment scene into a courtroom of hope and grace.
*   **Enmity as a Divine Gift (*ve-eyvah ashit*):** By yielding to temptation, humanity entered into natural alliance and harmony with Satan against God. Left to itself, the human heart would remain in willing subjugation to the prince of darkness. God announces: "And *I will put* enmity (*ve-eyvah ashit*)." Enmity against sin is not native to fallen human nature; it is a supernatural, grace-implanted hatred of evil placed by God in the soul.
*   **The Seed of the Woman and the Singular Pronoun (*hu yeshufkha rosh*):** The prophecy outlines two conflicting lineages: "between thy seed [the agents and spirit of rebellion, John 8:44] and her seed." The climax shifts from plural lineages to an individual Victor: the Hebrew masculine singular pronoun *hu* ("he / it", untagged in KJV-osis but present in WLC) specifies that the Seed of the woman will execute the decisive blow: *hu yeshufkha rosh* ("He shall bruise thy head"). While the serpent inflicts a painful but non-fatal wound upon the Messiah's heel (*ve-attah teshufennu aqev*, pointing to the suffering and physical death of Christ on the cross), the Seed delivers a mortal, crushing stroke to the serpent's head (*rosh*), guaranteeing the total overthrow of Satan and his dominion (Heb 2:14).
