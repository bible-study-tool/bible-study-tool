*   **Drove Out (*va-yegaresh et-ha-adam*):** Verse 24 uses stronger language than verse 23: God *drove out* (*va-yegaresh*, Piel of *garash*, H1644, used of forceful eviction, divorce, or expulsion). Fallen humanity could no longer abide in the immediate unshielded presence of holy Eden.
*   **Sanctuary Typology at the East Gate (*mi-qedem le-gan-eden*):** God placed (*va-yashken*, H7931, the root of *mishkan* / tabernacle and *shekinah*) at the east of the garden of Eden Cherubim (*ha-keruvim*, H3742) and a flaming sword (*lahat ha-cherev ha-mithappekhet*).
    1. *The Eastward Orientation:* The entrance of Eden, like the tabernacle and temple afterward, faced east.
    2. *The Cherubim:* Heavenly guardians of divine holiness and the throne of God (Exod 25:18–22; Ezek 10:1–22).
    3. *The Meeting Place of Worship:* As noted by Ellen White, the radiant glory between the cherubim at the garden gate served as the primeval sanctuary where Adam and his descendants brought their sacrificial offerings to meet with God until the Flood.
*   **Keeping the Way (*li-shmor et-derekh ets ha-chayyim*):** The verb *shamar* (H8104, "to keep/guard") was the exact duty entrusted to Adam in Genesis 2:15 (*le-shomrah*). Having failed to guard Eden from the tempter, holy angels now guard the way to the tree of life against unholy human intrusion. Yet in the gospel, Christ consecrated "a new and living way" through the veil of His flesh (Heb 10:20), restoring the redeemed to the tree of life in the paradise of God (Rev 2:7; 22:14).
