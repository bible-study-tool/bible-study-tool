*   **The Direct Contradiction (*lo-mot temutun*):** Sensing the woman's wavering, the serpent advances from insidious doubt to blatant falsehood: *lo-mot temutun* ("Ye shall not surely die"). He adopts the identical emphatic construction used by God in 2:17 (*mot tamut*, infinitive absolute with finite verb, denoting absolute certainty) and prefixes the absolute negative *lo* (H3808).
*   **The Origin of Spiritual Deception:** In Christian and Adventist theology (NOTICE.md), this statement is identified as the primeval lie upon which spiritualism and doctrines of inherent human immortality are built. By contradicting the divine warning that sin leads to death, Satan substituted his own assurance of unconditional preservation. Christ refers directly to this primal deception in John 8:44, declaring that the devil "was a murderer from the beginning" and "the father of lies."
