## Study Notes

*   **The Day Boundary Formula:** "And the evening and the morning were the fifth day" (*wayehi-erev wayehi-voqer yom chamishi*). The recurring refrain (v5, v8, v13, v19, v23, v31) continues to anchor the sunset-to-sunset day that governs the biblical calendar and, in due course, the reckoning of the Sabbath "from even unto even" (Leviticus 23:32).
*   **The Ordinal Numeral (*chamishi*, H2549):** The fifth literal day of creation week. With Day 5, the second triad advances: the domain of the sky and the waters (Day 2) is now furnished with its inhabitants — the fowl of the air and the teeming life of the seas.
*   **Structural Symmetry (Day 5 Fills Day 2):** Just as Day 4 corresponded to Day 1 (the luminaries operationalizing the day/night division), Day 5 corresponds to Day 2 (the expanse and the divided waters of v6-8 are now populated by birds and sea creatures). The week's symmetry (1↔4, 2↔5, 3↔6) continues its march toward the seventh-day Sabbath (Gen 2:2-3).
