## Study Notes

<!-- AI-GENERATED -->
*   **The Triple *Bara* (H1254):** Verse 27 uses the divine creation verb *bara* three times — "God created man... created he him... created he them." In Genesis 1, *bara* appears in only three verses: v1, v21, and v27 (three times here). The poetic triple repetition is emphatic: the creation of humanity is a unique, direct divine act, not an emergent process.
*   **"In His Own Image" (H6754):** The *tselem* of v26 is here realized: "in the image of God created he him." The image is not an add-on but the very mode of humanity's origin — humanity is created *as* God's image-bearer. Genesis 5:1-3 will trace the transmission of this image through the generations, and the New Testament locates its restoration in Christ (Col 3:10; Rom 8:29).
*   **"Male and Female Created He Them" (H2145, H5347):** The image of God is borne jointly by male and female. The two are created in the one act of *bara* — the foundational text for the equal dignity of the sexes. Christ cites this verse as the basis for the marriage union (Matt 19:4-5), and Adventist reading has consistently stressed that the image is not gendered-restricted but shared.
