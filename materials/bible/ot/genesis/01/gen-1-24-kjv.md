## Study Notes

<!-- AI-GENERATED -->
*   **The Earth Brings Forth the Land Animals (*yatsa*, H3318):** "Let the earth bring forth" (*totse ha'arets*, "cause to go out"). As with the waters (v20), the earth is summoned to participate in producing its inhabitants — the beast (*chayyah*, H2416, "living creature"), the cattle (*behemah*, H929, "dumb beast"), and the creeping thing (*remes*, H7431). The threefold classification of land animals is established here and governs the later clean/unclean legislation (Leviticus 11).
*   **Nephesh Chayyah Continues (H5315 + H2416):** The living creature of the land is again *nephesh chayyah* — the same phrase (KJV "living creature," i.e. "moving creature that hath life" in v20) applied to the sea creatures (v20-21) and to humanity (Gen 2:7). The land animals share the same category of living, breathing being; the biblical "soul" is consistently the living creature itself, not an immortal essence (see the fuller discussion at gen-1-20).
*   **"After His Kind" (*le-min*, H4327):** The reproductive boundary is fixed a third time (v11-12, v21, v24). Each class of land animal reproduces after its own kind — the creational order that grounds the later "after their kinds" distinctions of the dietary and sacrificial laws.
*   **Apparatus Note:** The closing formula *wayehi-ken* — the verb *wayehi* (H1961, "and it was") and the adverb *ken* (H3651, "so") — is left untagged in scrollmapper KJV-osis tagging (documented in `correlations/apparatus-genesis1.json`, key `Gen.1.24`).
