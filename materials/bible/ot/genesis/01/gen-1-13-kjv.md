*   **The Day Boundary Formula:** "And the evening and the morning were the third day" (*wayehi-erev wayehi-voqer yom shelishi*). Reaffirms the sunset-to-sunset day boundary established on Day 1 (Gen 1:5) and maintained throughout the creation week.
*   **The Ordinal Numeral (*shelishi*, H7992):** Following *yom echad* (Day 1) and *yom sheni* (Day 2), *yom shelishi* marks the third literal day of creation week.
*   **Structural Synthesis of the First Three Days:** Day 3 completes the first triad of creation week:
    - **Day 1:** Creation of light and division of day/night $\rightarrow$ Furnished on **Day 4** (sun, moon, stars).
    - **Day 2:** Creation of the expanse and division of waters above/below $\rightarrow$ Furnished on **Day 5** (birds of the air, sea creatures).
    - **Day 3:** Emergence of dry land and plant life $\rightarrow$ Furnished on **Day 6** (land animals, humanity).
    This structural symmetry demonstrates intentional, systematic divine design rather than random evolutionary accretion.
