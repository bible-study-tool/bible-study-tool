## Study Notes

<!-- AI-GENERATED -->
*   **The Green Herb for All (H402, *oklah*; H3418, *yereq*):** The plant provision of v29 is extended to the entire animal creation — "every beast of the earth, and every fowl of the air, and every thing that creepeth." The food of the original creation is shared by all: humanity and the animals alike are, in the text's own depiction, vegetarians (an interpretive observation, consistent with the framing at gen-1-29). The peaceable kingdom of the beginning (cf. Isa 11:6-9) is the divine design, and its restoration is the prophetic hope.
*   **"Wherein There Is Life" (H5315, *nephesh*):** The phrase "wherein there is life" is literally "in which is a living *nephesh*" — the same living-soul language applied to the animals throughout Day 5 and Day 6. The animals are living souls who share in the creational provision.
*   **The Heaviest Omission Set (9 tokens):** This verse carries the largest omission set of the chapter — nine Hebrew tokens left untagged in scrollmapper KJV-osis: *wayehi* (H1961), four occurrences of *kol* (H3605, "every"), *ken* (H3651, "so"), *al* (H5921, "upon"), *asher* (H834, "which"), and *et* (H853) (documented in `correlations/apparatus-genesis1.json`, key `Gen.1.30`).
