## Study Notes

<!-- AI-GENERATED -->
*   **The Divine Command Formula:** "And God said, Let there be..." (*wayyomer Elohim yehi*). Following the creation of light on Day 1, Day 2 opens with the second divine fiat of creation week. Creation proceeds by the spoken word of God (Psalm 33:6, 9; Hebrews 11:3).
*   **The Lexical Centerpiece — *raqiya* (H7549):** The noun *raqiya* derives from the verbal root *raqa* (H7554), meaning "to beat out, spread out, stamp" (as metal is hammered into thin sheets, e.g., Exodus 39:3, Isaiah 42:5). The Greek Septuagint translated this as *stereoma* (firm, solid structure), which Jerome rendered into Latin as *firmamentum*, yielding the English "firmament" in the KJV. Modern lexical scholarship (e.g., TBESH "expanse") emphasizes the expansive atmosphere/space rather than a solid metallic vault. Per NOTICE.md, different interpretive frameworks exist: historical-critical models emphasize ancient Near Eastern vault concepts, while Adventist and conservative scholars understand *raqiya* phenomenologically as the atmospheric expanse providing an open biosphere between the surface waters and upper atmospheric reservoirs.
*   **Division of Waters (*badal*, H914):** The verb *badal* ("to divide/separate") recurs here from Day 1 (v4, light from darkness). On Day 2, it establishes a spatial division: "in the midst of the waters" (*betokh hammayim*, H8432 H4325) to divide waters from waters. God structures what was previously an unorganized deep (*tehom*, v2) into defined ecological zones.
*   **Apparatus & Tagging Alignment:** The Hebrew text of Genesis 1:6 contains two occurrences of the verb *hayah* (H1961: jussive *yehi* "let there be" and *viyhi* "and let it divide/be"), which are not separately tagged in scrollmapper's KJV-osis tagging (documented in `correlations/apparatus-genesis1.json`). This is a known tagging convention of the source, not a textual omission.
